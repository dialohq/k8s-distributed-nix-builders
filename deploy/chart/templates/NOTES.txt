The store serves NFS and authenticated gRPC entirely inside the cluster.
Builders keep native Nix metadata in their own PVCs.

Use standard Nix tooling inside a builder:
  kubectl exec -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -it {{ include "dn.name" . }}-builder-0 -- nix --version

Run online GC:
  kubectl exec -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} {{ include "dn.name" . }}-store-0 -- distributed-nix gc

PVCs survive uninstall. Never share a metadata PVC between pool members or force
replace a pod whose old process may still be running. Membership is persisted;
changing the replica count requires an explicit retirement protocol, not HPA.
